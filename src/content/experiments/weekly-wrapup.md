cardDetail: "I inherited a team that had never really been a team. Different projects, different managers over time, no shared identity. I started Weekly Wrap-Up -- 30-60 minutes every Friday to connect and learn something together. The centerpiece was Show & Share: someone brings an object or presentation about something personal. I saw a bird watching book, original paintings, fully animated slide decks. People who barely talked to each other started showing up for each other. That's the whole point."
